Places to Visit in Nepal

A photographic journey through Nepal's most breathtaking landmarks, from the snow-capped Himalayan peaks to historic durbar squares and peaceful lakes.

Mount Ama Dablam

Soaring at 6,812 meters, this iconic peak in the Everest region is famed for its steep ridges and beautiful shape.

Phewa Lake, Pokhara

A tranquil freshwater lake reflecting the lush hills and the Annapurna range, famous for its colorful wooden boats.

Bhaktapur Durbar Square

A UNESCO World Heritage Site filled with ancient Newari art, towering pagodas, and exquisite wood and metal carvings.

Bardia National Park

A massive protected wilderness in Nepal's plains, home to Royal Bengal tigers, one-horned rhinos, and wild elephants.

Mount Ama Dablam

A majestic peak in eastern Nepal, soaring alongside Everest. Revered as one of the world's most beautiful mountains for its sheer icy ridges.

Phewa Lake

The heart of Pokhara. A peaceful freshwater lake where colorful wooden boats float beneath the reflection of the sacred Machapuchare peak.

Bhaktapur Durbar

An ancient museum of art and history. Explore medieval red-brick temples, pagoda towers, and the famous wood-carved Peacock Window.

Bardia National Park

An untouched sanctuary in Nepal's western plains, hosting deep Sal forests and grasslands where the Royal Bengal tiger roams free.
